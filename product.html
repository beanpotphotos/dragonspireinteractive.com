Product

An AI game master for the whole table.

DragonSpire is building a fully autonomous AI game master: it narrates, rules on actions, runs monsters, tracks combat, and hands out loot and experience, with no human running the table. It is alpha, and a full unattended session is not yet proven. GMs who want to stay in charge can use Assistant mode, a feature where a human approves each move.

Who it is for

  • Game masters

    Assistant mode, a feature for GMs who want a co-GM: it handles rules, bookkeeping, monster turns and narration drafts, with every consequential step behind an approval card.

  • Players

    A game master on voice, on the web and inside Fantasy Grounds, who never rolls for or decides for your character.

  • Groups without a GM

    In development

    Autonomous mode, where DragonSpire runs the table with no human GM. This is the goal of the product. In development; not yet proven live.

Where you play

  • Fantasy Grounds Unity

    It reads the live combat tracker, sheets and maps, and acts with the ruleset's own functions. Every Fantasy Grounds command passes a validator before it runs, and returns a receipt after.

  • Discord voice

    A narrator voice and the manners to wait for a pause.

  • The web

    Chat with the GM, roll 3D dice, see your sheet, the party and the initiative order. No Fantasy Grounds needed.

Sign in with a Google account. Joining a campaign takes an invite or a developer's approval; to request a spot, email play@dragonspire.io.
Runs using free Fantasy Grounds Unity software locally, in a Discord voice channel, and/or on the web. Each component is optional. You can play just on the web. You can play with just Discord & FGU.

A game night

  1. The GM opens Fantasy Grounds; FGU Link connects to Cory. Players join Fantasy Grounds and Discord; Dee Emma says hello once.

  2. The GM picks a mode: Passive, Assistant or Autonomous.

  3. Players talk. Cory decides whether a line is meant for the GM, checks whose turn it is, and answers, aloud in Discord and in Fantasy Grounds.

  4. A fight starts. Monsters go on the combat tracker, players roll their own initiative, and Cory follows the tracker turn by turn, asking each player to roll and suggesting each monster's move for the GM to confirm.

  5. The last foe falls. Experience is paid once. The GM offers loot; loot a player simply claims is refused.

  6. A quiet table gets one gentle prompt. A long silence is treated as a break.

What the game master controls

  • Four modes: Disabled, Passive, Assistant, Autonomous.
  • Approval cards for consequential steps.
  • Monster actions are suggestions until confirmed.
  • Narration is held for release in Assistant mode.
  • An emergency stop clears everything.

Rules automation inside the tabletop

  • Cover
  • Movement budget
  • Difficult terrain
  • Opportunity attacks
  • Reactions Shield, Counterspell, Uncanny Dodge, Sentinel
  • Sneak Attack
  • Grapple
  • Lingering spell areas
  • Summons

All run with Fantasy Grounds' own functions.

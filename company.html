Founder

Mike

Mike is the founder of DragonSpire Interactive. He plays tabletop D&D with a regular home group on Fantasy Grounds Unity, and he started DragonSpire because the AI game masters he tried could not keep track of the rules, the dice or the battlefield. He designed DragonSpire as a system where tested code, live game state and databases run the game, and the AI handles the narration and the voices. He built it with his own table as the testing ground, and it is now in alpha.

How DragonSpire is built

Started as a home game that needed a game master and became a system. DragonSpire has been built in the open with AI coding agents under strict engineering rules: hexagonal architecture, tests before every push, and documentation as the source of truth.
